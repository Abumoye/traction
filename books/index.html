Chapter Two
The Boss Who Changes Her Mind Every Monday

Tunde had a private nickname for his CEO within six months of joining the fintech in Yaba. He called her NEPA. Her instructions took light like PHCN, here now, gone before you'd finished adjusting to it.

The product roadmap that was locked on Monday was scrapped by Wednesday. A payment-processor deal he'd spent three weeks negotiating died two days before signing, killed by a text that said only, it's not the right time anymore. He'd stay up getting a set of wireframes exactly right, proud of the work, and two days later learn none of it would ship.

There is a version of this story where Amaka is simply indecisive, a founder who talks a confident game in pitch decks and falls apart the moment a real decision needs holding. That version isn't entirely wrong.
