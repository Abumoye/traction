What Is Traction Outsourcing Recruitment Services?

Traction Outsourcing Recruitment Services is the recruitment service of Traction Outsourcing Limited and one of the recruitment agencies in Abuja serving businesses across Nigeria. We were built to solve one specific problem: getting Nigerian businesses and homes the right person, fast, and affordably. We maintain a verified community of ready-to-work candidates, from entry-level graduates to experienced professionals, so that when you need staff, you are not starting from zero, you are choosing from people who are already screened and available.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Traction Outsourcing Recruitment Services the same as ATH Recruiters or Abuja Talent Hub?
Yes. Traction Outsourcing Recruitment Services was previously known as ATH Recruiters, and Abuja Talent Hub is the name of its community of verified, ready-to-work candidates. It now runs directly under Traction Outsourcing Limited, with the same community and the same recruitment service.
Is Traction Outsourcing Recruitment Services an affordable recruitment agency in Nigeria?
Yes. Traction Outsourcing Recruitment Services is built to be one of the more affordable recruitment agencies in Nigeria, with plans starting from ₦30,000 for select roles, keeping pricing accessible for small businesses and private individuals, not just large corporations.
Is Traction Outsourcing Recruitment Services a domestic staff recruitment agency in Nigeria?
Yes. Traction Outsourcing Recruitment Services works as a domestic staff recruitment agency in Abuja and across Nigeria, placing housekeepers, cooks, drivers, nannies, and other personal staff for private homes and individuals who need people they can trust.
How fast can Traction Outsourcing Recruitment Services fill a role?
Turnaround depends on the plan. Plan A offers a 24 to 48 hour turnaround for subscribers, Plan B typically delivers qualified candidates within 2 weeks for specialised roles, and Plan C is a fully managed process suited to leadership and bulk hires.
