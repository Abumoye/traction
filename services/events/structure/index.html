Why This Class

Most businesses do not restructure for fun. They restructure because growth has outpaced the structure, reporting lines exist on paper but not in practice, or nobody can explain who actually decides what.

This free online class lays out the basis of organizational restructuring: how to recognise the signs, what a properly rebuilt structure is made of, and how to start preparing your business for 2027 before the new financial year is already underway.

What the Class Covers

Signs You Need to RestructureGrowth outpacing structure, unclear reporting lines, and decisions that stall.
Governance and Decision RightsWho decides what, and where authority actually sits.
Organograms and Reporting LinesRedrawing the structure so it matches how the business really runs.
Roles, JDs and KPIsDefining roles with clear accountability, not inherited job titles.
Policies and Performance SystemsStaff policy and performance measures that fit the new structure.
Planning Ahead for 2027Where to start, and how to roll a restructuring out without disrupting the business.

Who Should Attend

Business Owners and CEOsLeaders whose business has grown beyond its original structure.
HR and Operations LeadersPeople responsible for roles, reporting lines and policy.
Founders and Managing DirectorsTeams preparing to scale, take on investment or merge entities.
