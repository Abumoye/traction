Common Questions

What types of corporate training does Traction Outsourcing offer?

Traction Outsourcing Limited offers professional development and corporate training across people management, HR practice, financial management for non-finance managers, customer service, and leadership development. Training is delivered as scheduled open programmes, in-house sessions for specific teams, and bespoke programmes built around a client's particular needs.

Do you offer in-house corporate training for companies in Nigeria?

Yes. Traction Outsourcing Limited delivers in-house training programmes for organisations that prefer to train their teams together in their own environment. In-house sessions can be adapted to the organisation's specific context, industry, and current capability level.

Where are your training programmes held?

Open programmes are held in Abuja and Lagos. In-house programmes are delivered at the client's premises anywhere in Nigeria. Some programmes are also available in hybrid or online formats depending on the scope and content.

How do I enrol my team in a Traction Outsourcing training programme?

Reach out through any of our contact channels, WhatsApp, email, or the enquiry form on this page. Share the number of participants, the preferred dates, and the area you want to develop, and our team will respond with the available options and how to proceed.
