Prepare Your Business for 2027

The Traction Outsourcing International Corporate Retreat is a premier yearly gathering crafted explicitly for CEOs, senior management executives, and ambitious entrepreneurs who take building institutional business structures seriously. This executive summit combines a leadership conference, business masterclass, and cross-border networking event into one immersive 9-day programme in Kigali, Rwanda. It is designed to step completely out of routine operational environments, bringing visionary leaders into close contact with billionaire mentors while exploring Africa's fastest-growing business hub.

Over the course of this highly immersive 2026 business event, you will participate in fireside chat sessions and masterclasses built to establish structural clarity, optimize workforce management strategies, and secure cross-border insights to solidify your enterprise layout ahead of the 2027 financial year. Registration for the T-ICR 2026 is open to business leaders from Nigeria and across Africa. Tickets are available until the event sells out.

Common Questions

What is the T-ICR 2026?
The T-ICR 2026, Traction Outsourcing International Corporate Retreat 2026, is an executive retreat and business conference taking place in Kigali, Rwanda from November 22 to 30, 2026. It brings together Nigerian and African business leaders for structured programming, speaker sessions, business matchmaking, and an immersive experience in one of Africa's most dynamic business cities.
Who should attend the T-ICR 2026?
The T-ICR 2026 is designed for CEOs, founders, senior executives, and business owners who want to combine high-quality business learning and networking with an international experience. Seats are allocated on a first-come basis and close once the event is full.
What does the T-ICR 2026 registration fee cover?
The registration fee is NGN 3,550,000 or USD 2,600 at the Early Bird rate, limited to the first 30 registrations and ending August 31st, 2026, or NGN 5,000,000 / USD 3,665 at the Standard rate afterward. Registration closes November 1st, 2026. It covers accommodation for the full retreat period, return flights (Abuja/Lagos to Kigali and back), programme fees, speaker sessions, business matchmaking, group activities, feeding, and ground logistics in Kigali. Full details are available on request.
How do I register for the T-ICR 2026?
Registration closes November 1, 2026. To secure your place, reach out via WhatsApp on 0704 708 2697 or email partner@tolnigeria.com. Seats are allocated on a first-come basis and close once the event is full.
