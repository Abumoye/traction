How to Recruit Qualified Staff in Nigeria in 48 Hours for Just ₦30,000

Traction Outsourcing Limited July 27, 2026 4 min read
Back to Articles

Recruitment in Nigeria should not take three months and cost a fortune. Most businesses are not looking for a unicorn. They need a competent, reliable person who can do the job well and show up consistently. ATH Recruiters was built exactly for that, and it is accessible to any business in Nigeria regardless of size.

If you have used a traditional recruitment agency in Nigeria before, you probably remember the experience. You submit a brief and then wait. Weeks pass. You follow up. More waiting. Eventually a batch of CVs arrives, half of them irrelevant. You shortlist three people for interviews, one shows up, and you end up settling because the process has already taken too long. Meanwhile the role has been vacant for two months and the business has been managing without it.

That is not how recruitment has to work. And for businesses using ATH Recruiters, it does not work that way at all.

Why the Turnaround Is 48 Hours

The reason most recruitment agencies are slow is that they start sourcing candidates after you submit your brief. They post on job boards, collect applications, and then begin the process of sorting through hundreds of responses, most of which will not be suitable.

ATH Recruiters works differently. The talent pool is built and maintained continuously. Candidates are sourced, vetted, and kept ready before any brief comes in. When you tell us you need a customer service officer, a warehouse supervisor, a PA, or a driver, we are not starting from zero. We are matching your brief against people we already know, have already spoken to, and have already confirmed are available and qualified.

That is what makes 48 hours possible. Not a shortcut on quality. A different starting point entirely.

The Three Plans

ATH Recruiters operates on three tiers, designed to match where your business is and what it needs.

  • Plan A — Fast Track Subscription. For businesses that hire regularly. Unlimited candidate requests during your subscription period, 24 to 48 hour turnaround on standard roles. Starting from ₦30,000 for select roles.
  • Plan B — Specialist Placement. For specific roles that need more careful matching. A structured search with a focus on skills verification and role fit. Typically two weeks for specialist placements.
  • Plan C — Fully Managed Search. For leadership roles, bulk hiring, and complex mandates. A fully managed process from brief to onboarding, with training included for your new team members.

Full plan details, scope, and current pricing are available at the ATH Recruiters plans page. If you are not sure which plan fits your situation, the easiest thing to do is reach out directly and we will advise you in under five minutes.

The Community Behind the Speed

The 48-hour turnaround is only possible because of what happens before you ever contact us. ATH Recruiters operates a private community of job seekers across Nigeria, and this community is not just a database of CVs. Members are actively trained, mentored, and prepared for the workplace through structured programmes run by our team.

This means the candidates you receive are not just available. They are ready. They have received guidance on workplace expectations, communication, professional conduct, and role-specific preparation depending on their target field. When you hire through ATH Recruiters, you are not taking a chance on someone who sent in a CV last week. You are meeting someone who has been part of a professional development community and is genuinely prepared to contribute from day one.

The community is what separates ATH Recruiters from a job board. The training and mentorship is what separates our candidates from a random CV pile.
