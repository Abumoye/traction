How to Know When You Need Traction Outsourcing

Traction Outsourcing Limited October 29, 2026 6 min read
Back to Articles

In Nigeria, most business owners do not wake up one morning and decide they need a consultant. For most, the need arrives slowly through years of ignored cracks in business processes. A resignation that should have gone smoothly turns into a 3-week crisis. A client disagrees with an invoice and nobody can find the signed agreement. A good month ends with an empty account, and nobody can say where the money went. The CEO handles each problem as it comes, usually late at night, until keeping the business alive becomes a full-time job of its own.

At Traction Outsourcing, we meet owners at this point every other week. Some run a hotel in Abuja or a real estate company in Port Harcourt. Others run a business in Nigeria while working full time in Ontario. The details differ, but the warning signs look terribly similar. Let us see three of the most common signs that you need Traction Outsourcing, what we do about each, and what usually changes once our work is done.

Sign 1: The Business Slows Down Whenever You Step Away

Travel for two weeks and switch off your phone in the evenings, then see what happens. If many Nigerian CEOs try this, approvals will pile up, staff will wait for instructions that may never come on time, and suppliers who should have been paid on Monday will still have no communication by Friday.

This usually happens when a business has grown around one person instead of around a system. Every decision passes through the founder because nobody else has the authority, the procedure or the confidence to make it. While owners may see this as commitment, it gradually becomes the ceiling on how big the business can grow.

At Traction Outsourcing, our Operations and Strategy team maps how work actually moves through your business, then formulate process documents that help to guide flow of work. We take time to understand your business and set delegated authority, so each manager knows exactly what they can approve without calling you. When our findings show that the gap is in people, our HR and Leadership team recruits managers who can carry responsibility and trains the ones you already have. The result? Your business keeps running at the right pace while you travel, leaving you time to plan for growth and expansion.

Sign 2: Contracts and Compliance Are Handled Loosely

In 2026, many business deals still close over a handshake or WhatsApp messages. Yes, nothing may go wrong until something does, and then it costs the business time, money and relationships. Imagine a client refuses to pay the balance and there is no signed agreement, or the agreement was loosely written. Imagine a staff member resigns with your client list and proprietary information, and nothing in their contract or company policy said they could not.

Our Legal team takes compliance seriously. Both our in-house team and partner firms follow our strict policies that ensure businesses like yours are fully protected. We audit your corporate documents, draft and review your contracts and staff documents, bring company filings up to date and liaise with government agencies. When a dispute arises, we manage resolution through the appropriate channels saving you years of litigation and court processes. The result? Your business becomes renewed and rebranded from inside out. You can continue to carry on business with confidence, knowing full well that your agreements can be enforced.

Sign 3: The Business Is Growing Faster Than Its Structure

Growth is good news until your sales double and the way the business runs stays the same. Five staff become thirty, yet there is still no organogram or reporting structure. One office quickly becomes three, but when customers walk in, each branch feels like a different company with inconsistent manners and procedures. Revenue rises, yet nobody has the data to show which product or branch actually makes the money, and when.

For businesses like this, Traction Outsourcing brings the structure up to the size of the business. Our Operations and Strategy team redraws reporting lines and builds HR systems for payroll, leave and performance that work the same way in every branch. Our Finance team sets up proper bookkeeping, prepares budgets and produces monthly management accounts, or trains whoever is on your team to do so.

With You in Person, or From Anywhere in the World

Often, we find that a problem in one area spills into other areas of the business. This is why our consultants provide 24/7 advisory, whatever your time zone and wherever you run your business from. Our consultants work on site with clients in Abuja, Lagos, Nasarawa, Kaduna, Ibadan, Enugu, Ogun, Ondo, Cross River, Bayelsa, Delta and Edo.

Many clients, however, do not need us in the same location for the whole engagement, or at any point during it. We have worked with dozens of businesses and hundreds of business owners without meeting them in person, from Nigerians in the US, the UK, Canada, Russia and Australia who run businesses back home, to business owners on holiday in Ghana, Rwanda or South Africa. When a CEO or business manager chooses remote support, they receive the same service and the same quality of result as a client we visit physically.
