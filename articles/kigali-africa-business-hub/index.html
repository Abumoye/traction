Kigali: Africa's Cleanest City, Rising Business Hub, and the Destination Every Nigerian Executive Should Know

Traction Outsourcing Limited July 23, 2026 4 min read Updated September 29, 2026
Back to Articles

Kigali has been quietly building something that very few African capitals can credibly claim. The city has become a genuine destination for business, investment, and international conferences like the Traction Outsourcing International Corporate Retreat 2026 (T-ICR 2026).

The Cleanest City in Africa

According to The Africa Report's 2025 Clean Cities Ranking, Kigali topped the list with a score of 9.4, with Rabat, Morocco, coming in a distant second.

Why the City Stays Clean

The cleanliness is structural. Rwanda banned plastic bags in 2008, imposing heavy fines for offenders. The last Saturday of each month is then dedicated to city cleanliness through Umuganda, a national community service tradition that involves everyone from ordinary citizens to the President.

What a Nigerian Executive Notices First

For a Nigerian executive visiting Kigali for the first time, this may come as a shock. When Prince Elijah Abumoye, CEO of Traction Outsourcing Limited, visited Kigali, this is how he described it:

"The roads do not have potholes, the streets do not have litter, and the air does not carry the smell of open waste. I walked through the inner roads and the result remains the same. Small stalls on untarred roads still keep the no-plastic rule. It was an amazing experience for me."

Kigali's Business Growth: The Numbers Behind the Story

Rwanda's GDP grew by 9.4% in 2025, according to the National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda, making it one of the fastest-growing economies on the continent. Kigali alone contributes over 40% of Rwanda's GDP.

A Pro-Business Environment

Rwanda is also pro-business. The Rwanda Development Board operates a One Stop Centre where businesses can register in a few hours, and the country allows 100% foreign ownership across key sectors.

The Kigali International Financial Centre (KIFC) aims to attract foreign companies and stimulate regional economic growth, positioning Rwanda as the East African Community's financial gateway.

A Gateway to East Africa

For Nigerian founders and executives eyeing East Africa as a market, Kigali offers easy access to the East African Community, a bloc of over 300 million people, with some of the least bureaucratic market entry conditions in Africa. Frequently Asked Questions

Is Kigali the cleanest city in Africa?
Yes. Kigali topped The Africa Report's 2025 Clean Cities Ranking with a score of 9.4, ahead of Rabat, Morocco. Its cleanliness comes from a plastic bag ban since 2008 and Umuganda, the monthly national clean-up day.
Why is Kigali considered a business hub?
Rwanda's economy grew by 9.4% in 2025. Businesses can register in a few hours at the Rwanda Development Board One Stop Centre, foreign investors can own 100% of companies in key sectors, and the Kigali International Financial Centre serves as a gateway to the East African Community market of over 300 million people.
