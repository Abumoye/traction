Running a Business in Nigeria From the UK, US, or Dubai? Here Is How to Build a Team Without Being There

Traction Outsourcing Limited July 27, 2026 3 min read
Back to Articles

You have the business idea, the capital, and the drive. What you do not have is a reliable way to build a team in Nigeria from six thousand kilometres away. You are not alone in that problem, and it is more solvable than you think.

Whether you are running a food and beverage brand from London, managing a property portfolio from Dubai, or building a services company from Toronto, the challenge is the same: Nigeria is where the business is, but you are not.

The good news is that your physical location does not need to determine how well your Nigerian business runs. It just determines how you set it up.

The Problem With Hiring Directly From Abroad

Most diaspora business owners start by hiring through personal connections, a cousin, a referral from a friend, someone a family member vouches for. Sometimes it works. Often it does not, and when it does not, the consequences land on you from thousands of miles away with limited ability to act fast.

The other approach is posting on job platforms and conducting WhatsApp interviews. This can surface candidates, but it does not solve the underlying problem: you have no way to verify who you are actually hiring, no HR infrastructure to onboard them properly, and no payroll or compliance process to keep everything legal.

A Real Example

A client based in the UK launched a bubble tea business in Abuja. Within three months of opening, she had gone through four managers hired through personal introductions. Each one left or underperformed, taking institutional knowledge with them. She had no employment contracts, no probation structure, and no one on the ground managing the HR process. By the time she came to us, she had lost nearly six months of operational momentum. We placed a vetted operations lead within a week, built out her staffing structure, and took the payroll off her plate entirely. The business is now running without her needing to be in Abuja to hold it together.

What You Actually Need

There are two distinct things a diaspora business owner needs when building a team in Nigeria, and conflating them is where most people get stuck.

The first is sourcing, finding the right people for the right roles. This requires a talent pool, a vetting process, and knowledge of the Nigerian job market. It is not something you can do reliably from a different time zone using generic job boards.

The second is infrastructure, employment contracts, payroll, statutory deductions, leave management, and the ongoing HR process that keeps your team legally employed and operationally accountable. Without this, even a great hire can become a liability.

Most diaspora founders try to handle sourcing themselves and skip the infrastructure entirely. That is usually where things go wrong.

The Roles You Can Hire For

From abroad, you can hire for virtually any role in Nigeria with the right support in place. The most common categories we handle for diaspora clients include:

  • Operations Staff
  • Sales & Customer Service
  • HR & Admin
  • Domestic Staff & Drivers
  • COO / Chief of Staff
  • Head of Operations
  • Legal Staff
  • Senior Management

The level of complexity changes depending on the role, but the process does not have to change for you. Whether you need a housekeeper for a property in Abuja or a Chief of Staff for a growing Lagos operation, the sourcing, vetting, and onboarding can all be handled on your behalf.

What is staff outsourcing and why do diaspora business owners use it?
Staff outsourcing means a third-party firm employs your Nigerian team on your behalf, handling their contracts, payroll, tax, and statutory deductions (ITF, NSITF, PenCom), while you direct their work. Diaspora business owners use it because it removes the need to set up a full HR function in Nigeria and ensures compliance with Nigerian labour law without needing to be physically present.
