Running a Business in Nigeria From the UK, US, or Dubai? Here Is How to Build a Team Without Being There

Traction Outsourcing Limited July 27, 2026 3 min read Updated October 13, 2026
Back to Articles

You have the business idea, the capital, and the drive. What you do not have is a reliable way to build a team in Nigeria from 6,000 km away. This problem is more common than you think, and your friends may be thinking about it too.

More Nigerians are investing in businesses in Nigeria with the aim of having a successful fall-back option when they decide to come back home. For some others, they are carrying a generational business that has stayed in the family for years. However, the challenges are universal: Nigeria is where the business is, but you are not.

The good news is that your physical location does not need to determine how well your Nigerian business runs. It just determines how you set it up.

The Problem With Hiring and Managing Directly From Abroad

Most diaspora business owners start by hiring through personal connections, a cousin, a referral from a friend, someone a family member vouches for. Sometimes it works, other times it does not. But when it does not work, the consequences are too expensive.

Another area is managing the staff well. Most Nigerians in diaspora hire a manager who is expected to manage the business like the owner. This does not work 8 times out of 10, and the reason is not far-fetched. The truth is that management without oversight gives room for excesses like stealing, copyright infringement, staff harassment, abuse of power, and many other workplace issues.

What You Actually Need

There are 3 important areas to focus on when you start building a team in Nigeria from abroad.

The first is recruiting the right people for the right roles. This requires having access to the right employee channel, a vetting process, and knowledge of the Nigerian job market for competitive compensation and benefits. It is not something you can do reliably from a different time zone using generic information on the internet.

The second is job infrastructure or documentation. This involves standardized legal offer letters, employment contracts, NDAs, payroll, statutory deductions, leave management, and the ongoing HR process that keeps your team legally employed and operationally accountable. Without this, even a great hire can become a liability.

The third is daily, weekly, and monthly staff management and leadership oversight. You need a tool that allows your management to measure staff value addition daily, weekly, and monthly. Amongst other things, the tool must also help you see how much value your team is giving you compared to how much you are investing in them.

What is staff outsourcing and why do diaspora business owners use it?
Staff outsourcing means a third-party firm employs your Nigerian team on your behalf, handling their contracts, payroll, tax, and statutory deductions (ITF, NSITF, PenCom), while you direct their work. Diaspora business owners use it because it removes the need to set up a full HR function in Nigeria and ensures compliance with Nigerian labour law without needing to be physically present.
What types of roles can I hire for in Nigeria from abroad?
Operations staff, sales and customer service teams, HR and admin personnel, domestic staff including housekeepers, cooks, and drivers, and senior management including COOs, Chiefs of Staff, Heads of Operations, and legal staff. Both junior and senior roles can be sourced, screened, and placed remotely.
