Common Questions

How do I rent a house through Traction Properties?
Reach out through the WhatsApp link on this page with your budget, preferred location, and number of bedrooms. Our team will share matching listings, arrange a viewing, and guide you through the tenancy agreement and move-in process.
Are the rental listings on this page the full list?
No, this page shows a sample of available homes. Our full inventory changes frequently, so contact us directly for the most current list of houses for rent in Abuja.
Does Traction Properties verify landlords before listing a rental?
Yes. We verify property ownership and documentation before a home is listed for rent, and we manage the tenancy agreement so both tenant and landlord are protected.
What areas of Abuja do you have rentals in?
We manage and broker rentals across Abuja, including areas such as Kado, Mabushi, and Wuse. Contact us with your preferred location and we will match you with available homes.
What does the property ID on a listing mean?
Every fully verified listing — one with confirmed photos, price, and location — carries a unique property ID badge, such as 104AB. You can search for a specific listing by typing its ID into the search box above the listings. Listings still being finalized do not yet have an ID.
